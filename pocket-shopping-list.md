## Substitution rules

**Item 1 (the board):** buy the first of these that's in stock:
1. **5483**: ESP32-S3 TFT Feather (preferred)
2. **5691**: ESP32-S3 Reverse TFT Feather. Same price; currently out of stock.
3. **6303**: ESP32-S3 Reverse TFT with w.FL Antenna. Same price. **Only if 1 and 2 are both out of stock.** If you buy this one, also add one Adafruit **2.4GHz antenna with a w.FL connector** (not u.FL). It won't get Wi-Fi without one.

If none of the three are in stock, **stop and ask me**. Don't pick a different board.

**Item 2 (the battery):** if 258 is out of stock, buy **1578** (Lithium Ion Polymer Battery
3.7v 500mAh, $7.95) instead. Any substitute battery must:
- be a 3.7V LiPo / Li-ion
- use a **JST-PH 2-pin** connector
- be **250mAh or larger**

## Do NOT buy
- Any Raspberry Pi, RGB LED matrix panel, Matrix Portal, or Bonnet/HAT. Those are for a different project.
- A USB-C cable. I already have one.
- Batteries with any connector other than JST-PH 2-pin.
- Extended warranties, gift wrap, or other add-ons.
